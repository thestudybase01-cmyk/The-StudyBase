Parents Want To Know

Here are the answers to some of our most frequently asked questions.

📚 What grades do you tutor?

We support learners from Grade 4 to Grade 12 with academic guidance, subject support, study skills and exam preparation.

📖 Which subjects do you offer?

We assist with a variety of subjects including Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy, English, Afrikaans, Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Geography, History, Economics and EMS.

💜 Do you support learners with ADHD or learning challenges?

Yes. We understand that every learner learns differently.

We provide patient, personalised support and adapt our approach to learners who may experience ADHD, concentration challenges, different learning needs or study difficulties.

💰 What are your tutoring fees?

Individual tutoring:
R300 per hour

Small group tutoring for two learners:
R250 per learner per hour

👥 Why choose small group tutoring?

Small groups give learners the opportunity to interact and learn together while still receiving individual guidance and personal attention.

📝 How do I register my child?

Simply complete our online registration form and we will contact you to discuss your learner's needs, availability and the most suitable tutoring option.

📍 Where are you located?

The StudyBase is located in Northmead, Benoni, Gauteng.

⏰ When are lessons available?

Lessons are available during weekday afternoons, with selected Saturday sessions available by appointment.
