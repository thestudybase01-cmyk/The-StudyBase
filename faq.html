Parents Want To Know

📚 What grades do you tutor?

We support learners from Grade 4 to Grade 12 with academic guidance, subject support, and exam preparation.

📖 Which subjects do you offer?

We assist with a variety of subjects including Mathematics, English, Afrikaans, Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Geography, History, Economics, and EMS.

💜 Do you support learners with ADHD or learning challenges?

Yes. We understand that every learner learns differently. We provide patient, personalised support and adapt our approach to help learners with concentration challenges, different learning needs, and study difficulties.

💰 What are your tutoring fees?

Individual tutoring:
R300 per hour

Small group tutoring (2 learners):
R250 per learner per hour

👥 Why choose small group tutoring?

Small groups allow learners to interact and learn together while still receiving individual attention and guidance.

📝 How do I register my child?

Simply complete our registration form or contact us directly through WhatsApp, phone, or email.

📍 Where are you located?

The StudyBase is located in Northmead, Benoni, Gauteng.

⏰ When are lessons available?

Lessons are available during weekday afternoons and selected Saturday sessions by appointment.
