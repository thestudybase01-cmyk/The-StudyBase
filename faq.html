Parents Want To Know

📚 What grades do you tutor?

Grade 4 to Grade 12.

📖 Which subjects do you offer?

Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy, English, Afrikaans, Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Geography, History, Economics and EMS.

💜 Do you support learners with ADHD or learning challenges?

Yes. We adapt our approach and provide patient, personalised support.

💰 What are your tutoring fees?

Individual: R300/hour
Small group: R250/learner/hour

👥 Why choose small group tutoring?

Learners can interact while still receiving individual guidance.

Complete the online registration form and we will contact you.

Northmead, Benoni, Gauteng.

⏰ When are lessons available?

Weekday afternoons and selected Saturday sessions by appointment.
